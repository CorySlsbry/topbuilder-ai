## Recording notes

- Use the **demo seed tenant** (run `pnpm seed:demo`) so the dashboard has realistic data: 6 active projects, 4 vendors with mixed COI status, 12 RFIs across phases, 3 change orders, 2 daily logs needing review, 6 AP bills awaiting coding.
- Record in Chrome with the Top Builder AI dashboard at full screen on a 1920×1080 display.
- For the Procore OAuth shots, use a sandbox account so we can show the consent dialog without live customer data.
- Captions: hard-burn-in via Premiere or DaVinci Resolve. Do not rely on YouTube auto-captions for the Procore submission.
- Music: skip. Procore reviewers prefer a clean spoken track.
- Upload to a public unlisted Vimeo or YouTube link, paste the URL into the submission form, and check "include captions."
